**Tips for choosing**:
- For **AAA studios and large-scale needs** — Aquent Games, Onward Play, or GameRecruiter often excel.
- For **international or full-team hiring** — 8Bit Agency, Values Value, or InGame Recruitment.
- The games industry values passion, portfolio/reel quality, and specific engine experience (Unity, Unreal, etc.). Specialized agencies understand crunch culture, layoffs cycles, and creative-technical balance better than general tech recruiters.
- Evaluate based on industry testimonials, placement success in your discipline (art vs. engineering vs. production), and flexibility (contract for project spikes is common).
- Check Clutch, Glassdoor, or industry awards, and verify their network in your target region or platform.

The video game talent market is competitive and project-driven. Contact agencies directly via their websites for current capabilities.
