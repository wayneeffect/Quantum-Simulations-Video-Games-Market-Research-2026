**Quantum simulation** is a key application within the broader **quantum computing** market (not a fully separate tracked market), while the **video game industry** is a massive, mature entertainment sector. Here are the latest available figures as of 2026.

### Quantum Simulation / Quantum Computing Market
Quantum simulation (modeling complex quantum systems for chemistry, materials, physics, etc.) drives much of the early value in quantum computing, alongside optimization and machine learning. The overall quantum computing market remains small but grows extremely rapidly due to heavy investment, government support, and advancing hardware/software.

- **Current Size (2025/2026)**: Estimates vary by source but generally range from **~USD 1.4–3.5 billion** for the full quantum computing market in 2025. Some reports place it around $1.5–2 billion in 2025/early 2026.
- **Projections**: 
  - By 2030: Commonly projected at **USD 18–20 billion** (with CAGRs of ~30–42%).
  - Longer term (2035+): Some forecasts reach $19–40+ billion by 2035, with broader economic impact (value creation) potentially in the **trillions** cumulatively due to applications in industry.
- **Notes**: Quantum software (including simulation tools) is a smaller subset, previously estimated in the low hundreds of millions and growing fast. Hardware, cloud services (QaaS), and applications like simulation are the main growth drivers. The market is still emerging, with significant upside tied to achieving practical quantum advantage.

### Video Game Industry Market
The video game market is one of the largest entertainment sectors globally, encompassing console, PC, mobile, and emerging areas like cloud/VR.

- **Current Size (2025/2026)**: Roughly **USD 190–330 billion** in 2025, with 2026 estimates around **USD 200–330 billion** (sources vary by inclusion of hardware, in-app purchases, etc.). Newzoo and similar reports often cite ~$189 billion for 2025, while broader analyses reach higher figures.
- **Projections**:
  - By 2030/2031: Commonly **USD 500–600+ billion**, with CAGRs around 6–13% depending on the report.
  - Longer term: Some optimistic forecasts approach or exceed **USD 900+ billion** by 2036.
- **Notes**: Mobile gaming dominates revenue share (~50%), followed by PC/console. Growth comes from live-service models, cloud gaming, esports, and new technologies (AI, AR/VR). The market is more mature and stable than quantum but faces cyclical challenges.

### Comparison and Context
- **Scale**: Video games are currently **~100x larger** than quantum computing and will remain so for years, as quantum is still in an early commercialization phase.
- **Growth**: Quantum computing shows much higher percentage growth (explosive CAGR) due to its nascent stage and transformative potential. Video games offer steadier, large-scale expansion.
- **Intersection**: Niche overlap exists in using quantum techniques for advanced game simulations/physics or educational quantum games, but it is not yet a meaningful market segment.

These figures are estimates from market research firms (MarketsandMarkets, Newzoo, Fortune, Grand View, etc.) and can vary based on methodology. The quantum market is particularly sensitive to breakthroughs in error correction and hardware scalability. For the most current data, check reports from Newzoo (games) or specialized quantum analysts.
