# Quantum Simulations vs Video Games: Market Research & Insights

**A comprehensive analysis of two dynamic tech ecosystems** — one exploding from the lab into industry, the other a mature entertainment powerhouse.

### 📊 What This Repository Covers

- **Market Size & Projections**  
  - Quantum Computing / Simulation: Current valuation and high-growth forecasts (2030–2035)  
  - Video Games Industry: ~$200B+ market and future outlook

- **Job Market Deep Dives**  
  - Entry, mid, and senior-level roles in both fields  
  - Required skills, certifications, salaries, and hiring trends  
  - Talent shortage in quantum vs. competitive landscape in games

- **Skills & Career Pathways**  
  - Recommended certifications (Qiskit, Unity, Unreal, etc.)  
  - Transition strategies between classical computing, quantum, and game development

- **Emerging Opportunities**  
  - Intersection areas: quantum-enhanced physics simulations, procedural generation, educational games, and scientific visualization

### Why This Matters
Quantum simulation represents one of the highest-potential near-term applications of quantum computing, while the video game industry continues to dominate entertainment and push real-time rendering, AI, and interactive technologies. This repo explores both markets side-by-side for researchers, developers, career switchers, and investors.

**Tech Stack / Tools Used**: Python, Pandas, Matplotlib/Plotly, Qiskit (for examples), Unity/Unreal references.
